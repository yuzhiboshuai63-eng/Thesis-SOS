## Usage Notes

1. **Context Matters**: Always ensure the synonym fits the specific academic context.
2. **Register Consistency**: Maintain formal academic register throughout.
3. **Avoid Overuse**: Don't repeatedly use the same replacement word.
4. **Collocation**: Pay attention to word combinations and collocations.
5. **Field-Specific**: Use discipline-appropriate terminology.
